Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with Wealth Path?

Download the app from Google Play or App Store, create an account, and follow the onboarding process. You'll be guided through setting up your first budget and financial goals.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes! Your data stays on your device by default. We use industry-standard encryption, and you have complete control over your information. Premium users can opt for encrypted cloud backup.

What's included in the free trial?

The 30-day free trial includes all Premium features: unlimited transactions, unlimited budgets & goals, financial literacy lessons, advanced analytics, cloud backup, and priority support. No credit card required.

How do I track my expenses?

Simply add transactions manually or link your bank account (where available). Wealth Path automatically categorizes your expenses and provides insights into your spending patterns.

Can I use Wealth Path on multiple devices?

Yes! With Premium or Lifetime plans, you can sync your data across all your devices using our secure cloud backup feature.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money), credit/debit cards, and bank transfers for Tanzanian users. International users can pay via credit card.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from the app settings or by contacting our support team. There are no cancellation fees, and you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with Premium within the first 30 days, contact us for a full refund.

What are financial literacy lessons?

Premium users get access to expert-created lessons on budgeting, saving, investing, and wealth building. These interactive lessons help you improve your financial knowledge while managing your money.

Still have questions?

Contact our support team at chingalo.family@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
